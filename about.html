The Story Behind CVCraft

Job searching is already stressful. Crafting an ATS-compliant resume should never require a predatory monthly subscription or sacrificing personal privacy.

Why We Built CVCraft

Almost everyone who has searched for a job in the digital era has encountered the same bait-and-switch: an online resume builder advertises itself as "Free to build your CV". You spend 45 minutes carefully inputting your educational credentials, employment milestones, and phone numbers. Only when you click Download PDF does a pop-up appear requiring an immediate \$19.99/month recurring charge.

We created CVCraft to offer a permanent, clean alternative:

  • No paywalls, ever: All templates, real-time styling controls, and PDF downloads are genuinely free.
  • No mandatory accounts: You don't need to surrender your personal email, create a password, or verify an inbox just to export a document.
  • No server tracking: We don't upload your employment history to any server. What you type stays inside your current browser session.

Our Privacy-First Architecture

Traditional software platforms operate heavy cloud databases that store candidate resumes. This creates a data liability: your address, phone number, salary markers, and career references could be exposed in data breaches or used for marketing profiling.

CVCraft takes an entirely different architectural approach. The application runs 100% client-side:

How your data is protected:

When you type in CVCraft, your text is stored in your computer's local browser memory (HTML5 localStorage). No API call sends your information over the internet.

When you export your resume, the PDF is synthesized directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript rendering engines, or formatted via your native browser print engine.

Open, Accessible Technology

CVCraft is built with lean web standards: semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 with custom properties, and vanilla JavaScript. We purposely avoid heavy server dependencies, closed proprietary frameworks, or paid third-party APIs.

This keeps the website exceptionally fast, lightweight on mobile data, accessible across all devices, and usable even in low-bandwidth regions worldwide.

Our Core Commitments

Honesty Over Hype

We do not use fake counters ("9 million resumes built"), fake review quotes, or misleading ad banners.

Recruiter Alignment

Templates are designed around genuine recruitment standards and automated ATS compatibility rather than gaudy visual gimmicks.
