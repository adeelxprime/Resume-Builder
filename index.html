Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our free CV builder, data handling, and export tools.

Is CVCraft really completely free?
Yes. All features—including all 4 resume templates, font customizations, section reordering, and unlimited PDF exports—are 100% free. There are no trial periods, watermarks, or hidden paywalls.
Do I need to create an account or sign up?
No account is required. You can start creating your resume right away. Your work in progress is automatically saved to your local browser storage so you don't lose progress if you accidentally refresh.
Where is my personal resume data stored?
Your resume data is stored exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. We do not operate a backend database and we never upload, transmit, or sell your personal details to any third-party server.
Are these templates ATS-friendly?
Yes. Our templates use clean semantic HTML hierarchy, standard section names (Experience, Education, Skills), and selectable text. We avoid complex multi-column tables and non-standard symbols that frequently trip up applicant tracking systems.
Can I create multiple resumes or download as PDF?
You can export as many PDFs as you need. If you are tailoring resumes for different job descriptions, you can edit your content, download a customized PDF, and adjust it anytime.
