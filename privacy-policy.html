Truth in Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 · We do not collect, store, or sell your personal resume information.

1. Summary of Our Privacy Philosophy

At CVCraft, we believe your career history, contact details, and qualifications belong solely to you. Unlike commercial platforms that require user registration and harvest resume details into central marketing databases, CVCraft is architected as an in-browser static web application.

Key Privacy Principle:

Your resume content is never uploaded to any CVCraft server or external database. All editing, rendering, and PDF processing occur locally on your own device.

2. How Resume Drafts Are Stored (Local Storage)

To prevent you from losing your work when you accidentally refresh or close your browser tab, CVCraft utilizes the web standard known as localStorage.

  • Scope: The data stored in localStorage remains entirely inside your current web browser on your device.
  • Retention: The draft persists on your local machine until you manually click the "Clear All" button in the builder or clear your browser's site data/cache.
  • Important Notice: Because data is stored in the local browser, clearing browser history, using Private/Incognito mode, or switching to another computer or device will not transfer your draft. We recommend downloading a PDF copy as your permanent backup.

3. Third-Party Libraries and External Resources

CVCraft uses a minimal set of reputable external web resources to render typography and generate PDF files:

  • Google Fonts: We link to fonts (Inter, Merriweather, Roboto) hosted by Google Fonts to ensure crisp typographic display across all operating systems. Google may collect standard web telemetry (such as your IP address) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): The client-side PDF export tool (html2pdf.bundle.min.js) is loaded via cdnjs/Cloudflare to ensure fast, secure content delivery. Cloudflare processes standard connection metadata to deliver these scripts securely.

Neither Google Fonts nor cdnjs receives any text, names, emails, or job histories that you enter into the CVCraft editor.

4. Advertising and Future AdSense Readiness

In the future, CVCraft may display contextual advertising via third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense to offset domain and hosting maintenance costs.

If and when advertising networks are active:

  • Advertising partners may use cookies or web beacons to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com) or through tools provided by the Digital Advertising Alliance.
  • At no time will your CV content, contact information, or form input be shared with advertisers.

5. Cookies and Web Analytics

CVCraft does not set proprietary tracking cookies. We do not maintain user profiles, fingerprinting scripts, or cross-site behavioral tracking beacons. Standard HTTP server logs from our static hosting provider may record routine technical diagnostic data (such as HTTP status codes, browser user-agent, and IP address) solely for network reliability and DDoS mitigation.

6. Data Security

Because we do not maintain a backend database containing candidate profiles, there is no central database of CVCraft user resumes that could be breached, leaked, or ransomed. However, because your draft is stored in your computer's browser, you should be mindful when using shared public computers (e.g., at libraries or internet cafes) and click "Clear All" before finishing your session.

7. Children's Privacy

CVCraft does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. High school and college students using the service retain full control over their own locally generated documents.
